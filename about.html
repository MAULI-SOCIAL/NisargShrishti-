Rooted In Passion

Our Story

Every journey begins with a deep connection to the roots.

Born and raised in a farming family here in Pune, my bond with the land was forged early in life. For my family, agriculture was not merely a profession; it was a way of living, observing, and respecting the rhythmic heartbeat of Mother Earth. Growing up, I learned to listen to the whisper of the winds through the fields, to appreciate the quiet hard work of the earth, and to cherish every creature—big or small—that calls nature its home.

What started as a personal passion and a peaceful retreat soon grew into a deeper vision. In a world moving at a frantic pace—where concrete landscapes replace open fields and screens replace the beauty of blue skies—I felt a compelling desire to pause and share the magic I had experienced with everyone else. I wanted children, students, families, and city dwellers to feel the quiet joy of sinking their feet into warm earth, breathing clean rural air, and reconnecting with the living world around them.

That dream took shape as Nisarg Shrishti Agro-Tourism.

Where Passion Meets Preservation

Located on the picturesque outskirts of Pune, Nisarg Shrishti is more than just a tranquil getaway; it is a living sanctuary created to inspire curiosity, learning, and environmental stewardship.

🌱 Exotic & Rare Flora

Walk through lush green trails populated by rare indigenous plant species, vibrant medicinal flora, and captivating exotic foliage.

🦜 A Vibrant Aviary

Listen to the symphony of nature. Our aviary houses fascinating native and exotic bird species, filling the morning air with colors and calls.

🐐 Animal Sanctuary

Experience warm, interactive moments with gentle farm animals in an environment designed with love, care, and respect.

📚 Learning Beyond Textbooks

We invite students and families for hands-on agricultural learning, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable farming practices.

Our Mission

At Nisarg Shrishti, our mission is simple yet profound: To educate, inspire, and protect.

We believe that true conservation begins with love, and love begins with experience. When a child feeds a bird, marvels at a rare blooming flower, or learns how a single seed grows into a bountiful harvest, a seed of respect for our planet is planted in their heart forever.

Whether you are looking to step away from the urban bustle for a day of peaceful relaxation, bring your children for an enriching educational experience, or simply immerse yourself in the vibrant colors of nature, Nisarg Shrishti opens its arms to welcome you.

"Come, step into our world. Feel the land, meet its living wonders, and leave with a heart full of joy and a renewed love for nature." — Sagar Pathare, Founder
